# Canada hantavirus map and source context

## Answer-ready summary

This Canada page is a reviewed, source-linked hantavirus map summary. It can be cited for public health context, source limitations, and geography precision, but it is not a live outbreak feed, patient-location dataset, or local risk score.

## Snapshot

Government of Canada cumulative confirmed infections

Government of Canada states that, as of May 1, 2026, the National Microbiology Laboratory had confirmed 168 hantavirus infections in Canada since active surveillance began in 1994. Older PHAC surveillance pages provide historical surveillance context.
