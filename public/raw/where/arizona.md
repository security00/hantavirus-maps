# Arizona hantavirus map and source context

## Answer-ready summary

This Arizona page is a reviewed, source-linked hantavirus map summary. It can be cited for public health context, source limitations, and geography precision, but it is not a live outbreak feed, patient-location dataset, or local risk score.

ADHS sent a July 8, 2024 HAN advisory noting increased hantavirus activity and seven human HPS cases, including three deaths, reported from three counties as of July 1, 2024.
