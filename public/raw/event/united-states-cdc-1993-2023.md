# United States hantavirus source-linked case summary

CDC state-level historical summary through 2023

## Summary

CDC reports 890 laboratory-confirmed hantavirus disease cases in the United States from the start of surveillance in 1993 through the end of 2023. CDC states public data are reported by state only and county-level data cannot be provided to protect identities.

## Safe interpretation

Not a real-time tracker. County-level data are not published here. Counts may differ from provisional weekly NNDSS tables.

This record is a reviewed public health map note. It is not live surveillance, medical advice, patient-location tracking, or county-level risk prediction.
