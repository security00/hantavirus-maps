# Canada response to MV Hondius Andes hantavirus event

Government of Canada / PHAC / Global Affairs Canada · Official response update

## Summary

Canadian federal public health updates described continued domestic and international coordination for the MV Hondius Andes hantavirus event, including self-isolation and monitoring for exposed travellers beginning on 10 May for a minimum of 21 days and up to 42 days, plus additional potentially exposed individuals in Canada.

## Safe interpretation
